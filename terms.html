Free trial

Paying

Payments are made online through Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking), or by UPI or bank transfer using the details we send on WhatsApp. Razorpay never shares card or bank details with us. Doorstep unlocks within minutes of the payment being confirmed, and an invoice is emailed to the address given.

Monthly plan: missed payments

If a monthly payment fails, Doorstep keeps working for 15 more days while the school sorts it out. After that, check-in pauses until the payment is made.

School data

The school’s records stay on its own devices and in its own Google Sheet; we never receive them. To check licences, the app sends us only the school code, the school name, an anonymous device code and the app version. See the Privacy Policy.
