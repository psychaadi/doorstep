Doorstep

Staff attendance, leave, salaries and safe child pick-up for preschools, from one device at the gate.

Check in by face or card

Staff look at the device or show a QR card. Late arrivals and permissions are worked out for you.

Leave and calendar

Teachers ask for leave on the device. Approve it yourself or let the app follow your rule.

Salaries done

Monthly pay is worked out from attendance and leave, behind your admin password.

Safe pick-up

Only the family's approved people, with a family PIN, can collect a child.

Your data stays yours. Records live on your school's devices and in a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive. We have no server and never see any of it. Faces are kept only as encrypted numbers that only your school's devices can unlock, and children's faces are never scanned.
