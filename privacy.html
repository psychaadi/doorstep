Privacy Policy

Doorstep · Last updated 24 November 2026

In short: the app has no server of its own. Everything a school records stays on the school's own devices and in a Google Sheet in the school's own Google Drive. We, the makers, never receive, see or store any of it. Faces are turned into numbers on the check-in device and never leave it.

Who we are

Doorstep is an Android app for preschools, made by Aadithya EdTech. It records staff attendance, leave and salaries, and the safe drop-off and pick-up of children. The school that installs it decides what is recorded and is responsible for that data (under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the school is the Data Fiduciary). The app is the school's tool; we do not process the data on the school's behalf because it never reaches us.

What the app records

  • Staff: name, role, phone number, shift times, joining date, birthday (optional), attendance times, leave requests, monthly salary and payslips.
  • Families: family name, parents and other approved collectors (name, relation, phone, birthday optional), children's names, class and birthday (optional), and each day's drop-off and pick-up times with who collected the child.
  • Consent records: when a person agreed, to which version of the consent text, and whether they agreed to face recognition.
  • Device notes: the device model and the time it last synced, so the school can tell if the check-in device stopped working.

Face recognition

  • Only staff and parents who give consent can use their face. Everyone else uses a QR card.
  • The device's camera image is turned into a set of numbers (a "faceprint") by software running on the device itself. No photo is saved, and no image or faceprint is ever uploaded, synced or sent anywhere.
  • Faceprints are stored encrypted, only on the device where the face was added (normally the check-in device).
  • Children's faces are never scanned or stored. Children are checked in and out by a parent or an approved collector.
  • When a person withdraws consent, or is removed, their faceprint is deleted: at once on the device where this is done, and on the check-in device as soon as it next syncs.

Where the data is kept

  • On the school's devices (the check-in device and the admin's phone), so the app works without internet. Salary data is encrypted on each device.
  • In the school's own Google Sheet, in the Google Drive of the Google account the school signs in with. The Sheet is private to that account unless the school chooses to share it. Google stores it under Google's Privacy Policy.

There is no other copy. We have no database, no server and no access to the school's Google account.

Google sign-in and what the app can see

The app asks Google for one permission only: "See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" (drive.file). It can open the Sheet it created and nothing else in the Drive. It cannot read Gmail, contacts, photos or any other file.

Doorstep's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to keep the school's Sheet and devices in step. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never shown to us, and never used to train any AI model.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No AI or cloud services: face matching runs offline on the check-in device.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

The only thing the app downloads from us is a small public file that says whether a newer version of the app is available. Nothing about the school is sent when it checks.

Keeping it safe

  • Admin screens, salaries and settings are behind an admin password, stored only as a salted hash.
  • Family pick-up can be protected by a PIN, and only names on the family's approved list may collect a child.
  • QR cards are signed, so a copied or made-up card is refused; a lost card can be cancelled.
  • All syncing with Google uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

How long it is kept, and deleting it

The school decides. Records stay until the school deletes them in the app or in its Google Sheet.

  • To delete everything: delete the Google Sheet from Google Drive and uninstall the app (uninstalling removes all data on that device, including faceprints).
  • To stop the app reaching the Google account: remove "Doorstep" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Staff and parents who want to see, correct or delete their data should ask the school, which can do it directly in the app or the Sheet.

Children

The app is used by schools, not by children. Information about children is entered by the school with the parent's consent, which the app helps the school record.

Changes
