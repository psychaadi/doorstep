Refunds and cancellations

Doorstep · Aadithya EdTech · Last updated 27 November 2026

In short: use the free 7-day trial to make sure Doorstep suits your school. If Doorstep doesn’t work on your devices and we can’t fix it within 7 days of setup, you get your money back in full.

Our working promise

If Doorstep does not work on the school’s devices and we cannot fix it within 7 days of setup, we refund the first payment in full.

Other refunds

  • Paid twice by mistake, or paid but the licence never arrived and we can’t fix it: full refund of the extra or failed payment.
  • If we ever stop offering Doorstep, monthly schools are not charged again and any month paid in advance but not provided is refunded.
  • Otherwise, payments are not refundable once the licence is active.

Cancelling the monthly plan

Stop any time, with no notice and no fee: cancel the Razorpay subscription from the link in your payment email, or ask us to. Doorstep keeps working until the end of the month already paid for. Part months are not refunded.

How refunds are paid

Ask us by email or phone (see Contact). Approved refunds are sent back to the same UPI, card or bank account through Razorpay within 7 working days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.
