Delivery

Doorstep · Aadithya EdTech · Last updated 27 November 2026

Doorstep is software. Nothing is shipped by post or courier.

  • The app is downloaded onto the school’s Android devices, and can be tried free for 7 days before paying.
  • The licence is delivered digitally. It reaches the school’s devices by itself within a few minutes of payment (at most an hour). In the app, Admin → Doorstep licence → Check again fetches it at once.
  • Receipt and invoice are emailed to the address given at payment.
  • Setup help, where it is part of the agreement, is given by phone, video call or a visit in Chennai, at a time agreed with the school.

If the licence hasn’t arrived within an hour of payment, please contact us.
